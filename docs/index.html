Quiet when you need it.

Mutes during calls, in fullscreen apps and in quiet hours. Per-app rules pick a scene or silence. Press F9 to pause from anywhere. Close the window and Keyfire keeps running in the tray.

  • AtmosphereRain, storm, highway, wind, night. Stack three beds, add your own loops.
  • Your musicPlay your tracks with Lo-fi, Night, Bass boost and more.
  • Focus timerOwn scene for focus and breaks, with a chime.
  • StatsLive WPM, streaks, achievements, on-screen keyboard.
  • ThemesTwelve themes, eight accents, dark, light or follow Windows.
  • ReliableGlobal hook, output picker, health panel, self-restart.
