What you get

A whole room for your keyboard.

Sound, mood and automation in one tray app. Close the window and it keeps firing.

Shotgun kit

Letters blast, Space booms, Enter pumps the action, Backspace drops a shell. Or a synthesized shotgun with no files at all.

Tone and feel

Deep thock, crisp, warm, vintage. Bass, treble, pitch, stereo width, per-key overrides, full-auto mode.

Atmosphere

Rain, storm with thunder on Enter, highway, wind, night. Stack three beds and add your own loops.

Your music

Play your own tracks with profiles like lo-fi, night and bass boost. Streamed, never loaded into memory.

Scenes

Range day, Canyon, Storm, Night watch, Rainy desk. One click loads sound, ambience and room together.

Stays out of the way

Auto-mute on calls, in fullscreen apps and during quiet hours. Pause anywhere with F9. Focus timer built in.

Only in Keyfire

It listens to you.

Type fast and the ambience swells, the music speeds up and brightens, and shots fire tighter. Slow down and everything relaxes. Turn on Follow my typing speed and the soundtrack moves with you.
