# Sound sources and licenses

## Bundled (CC0 / public domain)
From https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds (CC0 1.0). Levelled and re-encoded only.
- `assets/kit/`      shotgun-style shots and booms (see assets/kit/CREDITS.md)
- `assets/ambience/` recorded rain, storm (rain + thunder), distant highway, running water (see assets/ambience/CREDITS.md)

## Real recorded shotgun (CC0) to drop into the kit folder
Name files `blast-*`, `boom-*` (Space), `pump-*` (Enter), `shell-*` (Backspace / modifiers), then Settings > Open kit folder.
- Shotgun Reload Sound effects (zer0_sol, CC0): pump action + shells  https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects
- The Free Firearm Sound Library (CC0): shotgun, pump action           https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
- Shotgun: Shots (BigSoundBank, CC0, recorded at skeet shooting)       https://bigsoundbank.com/shotgun-shots-s0532.html
- Gunshot Sounds (OpenGameArt, CC0, includes a shotgun)                https://opengameart.org/comment/112637

  https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-background-ambience  and  https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-sounds

## Avoid if you plan to share or sell the app
- CC BY sources need attribution; "non-commercial" sources (e.g. freesoundeffects.com) can't ship in a product;
  packs that forbid redistribution (e.g. Magic Sound Effects on itch.io) can't be bundled.
