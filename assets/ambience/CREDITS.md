# Ambience credits

All files are from the "CC0 Public Domain Sounds" collection
(https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).
They were level-matched, converted to Ogg Vorbis, and (thunder) given a fade-out. Nothing else changed.
